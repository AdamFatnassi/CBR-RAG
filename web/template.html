    1 · Retrieve

    Distance between the new client and every stored client, one term per variable, weighted:

    distance = Σ wj·dj / Σ wj   similarity = 1 − distance

    dj = |a − b| for numeric variables (scaled to [0, 1] with the 1st and 99th percentiles of the training clients, values beyond are capped) and 0 / 1 for categories (same / different). The k most similar clients are kept; ties go to the earlier case.

    2 · Reuse

    Score = share of subscribers among the k neighbours, each neighbour counting its similarity (× its reliability, 1 unless the reliability Revise is used). π is the share of subscribers among all stored clients (about 11%).

    score = Σ wi·yi / Σ wi   wi = simi · reli   yes if score ≥ π

    The client is added to the case base with the real outcome and reliability 1. The next client can be compared with him. Nothing is retrained.

    Variable weights

    Share of the total weight per group of variables, for the three schemes compared on the validation set. Mutual information measures how much a variable tells about the outcome; it is computed on the training clients only. The 5 economic indicators are strongly correlated, so they share the weight of the best one instead of counting 5 times.
